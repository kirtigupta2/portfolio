My process

Understand the domain

Before I draw anything I learn what already exists, who it serves and what the business is trying to do. I read, use the competing products and talk to the people closest to the work, so I know the landscape before I question it.

Ask and observe

I ask simple questions and then stay quiet. I watch someone use a product and look for where they hesitate, work around something or say one thing and do another. What people do tells me more than what they say.

Find the gap

I put the observations side by side and look for patterns. Then I decide what is worth changing: the gap between what people need and what the business is aiming for, narrowed down to one thing I can act on.

Sketch, build and test

An idea becomes something someone can try: a sketch, then a prototype, then working code. I put it in front of people, watch what they do with it and take what I learn back into the next round.
